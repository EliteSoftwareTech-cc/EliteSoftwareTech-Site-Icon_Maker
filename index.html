Native C++ Multi-Resolution Windows Icon Studio

Icon Maker creates, edits, previews, and exports hybrid multi-resolution Windows .ico containers (256×256 PNG stream down to 16×16 32bpp DIB + 1bpp AND mask) from any WIC-supported image format, featuring border-aware background stripping, magic wand color keying, pixel touch-up brushes, and live 8-size previews.

Editing & Transparency Engine GDI+ & WIC

  • Auto-Strip Background: Border/corner flood-fill removal with anti-aliased edge feathering.
  • Magic Wand (Contiguous & Global): Sample and strip matching colors within tolerance.
  • Eraser, Paint Brush & Eyedropper: Manual 1px–64px pixel editing with full Undo/Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y).
  • Bicubic Resampling: Alpha-premultiplied downscaling across 256, 128, 96, 64, 48, 32, 24, and 16 px sizes.

Windows Native Architecture WIN32 • CMAKE

  • Full ICO Container Parser: Reads PNG and DIB icon frames (1, 4, 8, 24, 32 bpp) and promotes the largest frame to master canvas.
  • 20 Embedded Custom Cursors: Compiled directly from resources/Cursors/ (.cur & .ani).
  • Registry Persistence: Stores settings and window geometry under HKCU\EliteSoftware\IconMaker.
  • Headless Self-Test: Run IconMaker.exe --test to verify codecs, scaling, cursors, and registry persistence.
